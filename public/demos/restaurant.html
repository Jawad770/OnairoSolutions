Our Story

A Lahore tradition since 1998

Al-Noor started as a small dhaba on MM Alam Road, serving handi-cooked biryani to office workers and families alike. Today, we're one of Gulberg's most loved desi restaurants — but the recipes haven't changed.

Every karahi is cooked fresh in a wok. Our seekh kebabs are marinated overnight. And our raita? Made from scratch every morning.
