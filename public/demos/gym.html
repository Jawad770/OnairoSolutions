Nutrition Tips

Eat to perform

Simple strategies that work with desi meals — not against them.

Protein at every meal

Aim for a palm-sized portion of chicken, fish, eggs, dahi or lentils at breakfast, lunch and dinner.

Hydrate before you train

Drink 500 ml water in the hour before your session — performance and pumps improve immediately.

Carb-time your training

Put rice, roti or oats around workouts. Keep evenings lighter if fat loss is the priority.

Weekend damage control

Enjoy the biryani — then get back on plan Sunday night. Consistency beats perfection.
