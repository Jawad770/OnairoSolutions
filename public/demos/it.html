Case Studies

Deep dives into real outcomes

Horizon Fleet Hub

ChallengeDispatch relied on phone calls and spreadsheets — delays cost fuel and customer SLAs.
ApproachUnified web dispatch, driver Flutter app, and automated ETA notifications via SMS.
StackReact, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Redis, AWS ECS, Mapbox APIs.
Delivery14-week MVP, 8-week rollout to 240 vehicles, ongoing support retainer.
Results38% faster assignments, 22% fuel savings, NPS +18 points in six months.

Meridian Patient Portal

ChallengePatients waited on hold for results; staff re-keyed data between legacy EMR and front desk.
ApproachPatient self-service portal, HL7 integrations, and encrypted telehealth sessions.
StackNext.js, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, WebRTC, Azure Key Vault.
Delivery20 weeks including compliance review and phased clinic rollout.
Results62% fewer inbound calls, 4.8? app rating, 30% more online bookings.

AlNoor Lending Platform

ChallengePaper-heavy loan files slowed approvals and audit preparation for regulators.
ApproachDigital KYC, credit decision engine, and Sharia-compliant product configurators.
StackTypeScript, NestJS, DynamoDB, AWS Lambda, React admin.
Delivery24-week build with parallel security audit and pilot branch launch.
ResultsApplication time cut to 4 minutes, 3 — loan throughput, audit-ready logs.
