About Us

Excellence in every classroom

Greenfield Academy is a premier private day school committed to nurturing intellectual curiosity, moral integrity, and global citizenship. Our Cambridge-aligned curriculum is delivered by qualified educators in a supportive, inclusive environment.

From our founding in 2008 to today's purpose-built campus, we have remained dedicated to one goal: preparing students not merely for examinations, but for meaningful, successful lives.
