FAQ

Common questions

Quick answers before you message us on WhatsApp.

How long does it take to launch?

Most websites go live within 7–10 days from our first WhatsApp chat. Simpler one-page sites can be faster; larger multi-page projects may take a little longer depending on content and revisions.

Do I need to provide content and photos?

It helps if you can share your logo, business photos, and basic info (services, hours, location). If you're missing anything, we can guide you on what to send — and help write website text if needed.

Is domain and hosting included?

Website design packages cover design and development. Domain and hosting are quoted separately so you only pay for what you need — we'll recommend affordable, reliable options for your country.

What if I want changes after launch?

All packages include support via WhatsApp. Business and Premium plans include free support for 1–3 months. After that, small updates (menu changes, new photos, hours) are handled quickly at fair rates.

How do I pay?

We accept bank transfer and other common payment methods depending on your country. Typically 50% to start and 50% before launch — we'll confirm details on WhatsApp.

Will my site work on mobile phones?

Yes — every site is built mobile-first. We test on phones and tablets before launch so your site looks great and loads fast on any screen.
